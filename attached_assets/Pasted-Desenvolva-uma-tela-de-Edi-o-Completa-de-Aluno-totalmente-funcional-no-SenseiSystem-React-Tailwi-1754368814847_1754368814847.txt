Desenvolva uma tela de Edição Completa de Aluno totalmente funcional no SenseiSystem (React + Tailwind + Supabase), transformando o protótipo existente em um sistema completo com backend integrado.

🗄️ Backend/Database (Supabase)
Tabelas necessárias:


-- Tabela principal de alunos
students (
  id, first_name, last_name, birth_date, cpf, rg, gender, email, phone,
  emergency_contact_name, emergency_contact_phone, created_at, updated_at
)

-- Endereços
student_addresses (student_id, zip_code, street, number, complement, neighborhood, city, state)

-- Saúde e graduação  
student_health (student_id, parq_answers[], medical_observations)
student_graduations (student_id, current_belt, current_degree, last_promotion_date)

-- Responsáveis financeiros
financial_responsibles (student_id, relationship, name, cpf, email, phone, is_self_responsible)

-- Planos
student_plans (student_id, type, value, due_date, frequency, duration, observations)

-- Documentos
student_documents (student_id, document_type, file_url, status, uploaded_at)

-- Avaliações físicas
physical_assessments (student_id, assessment_date, height, weight, bmi, flexibility, strength, endurance, evaluator, observations)

-- Contratos
student_contracts (student_id, signed, signed_date, signed_ip, signed_by, contract_html)
